Malayalam Market Context

  • 🥇
    Canada overperforms dramatically. 10 Canadian metros generate $377K (33.8%) of total gross. Toronto alone ($231K) outpaces all 91 US metros except New York and Dallas-FW combined.
  • ⚡
    York Cinema is a phenomenon. A 6-screen indie in Richmond Hill, ON grossed $98K — more than entire metros like Seattle ($31K) or Washington DC ($25K). Pure diaspora density effect.
  • 📍
    Malayalam diaspora map. Rankings mirror Kerala community concentrations — Toronto, Bay Area, Dallas-FW, Houston, Chicago, Kitchener. These are not general Hindi film markets.
  • 🔍
    LA is genuinely mid-tier here. #15 overall, $17K across 11 locations. The LA Malayalam community is smaller than Toronto or even Kitchener despite LA being the largest US market.

    Top 5 metros = 56% of gross. Toronto, NY, Dallas-FW, SF Bay Area, Kitchener. Highly concentrated — a small number of diaspora-dense markets drive the film's performance.
